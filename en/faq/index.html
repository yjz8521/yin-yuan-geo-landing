YinYuan GEO FAQ

Straight answers about generative engine optimization (GEO) and YinYuan GEO.

What is GEO (generative engine optimization)?

GEO (Generative Engine Optimization) is the practice of getting your brand accurately mentioned and recommended in generative AI answers — across Chinese engines (Doubao, DeepSeek, Qwen, Zhipu, Baidu ERNIE, Kimi, Yuanbao) and global ones (ChatGPT, Perplexity, Google AI Overviews/Gemini, Claude, Copilot). The core is consistent, verifiable, easy-to-cite brand facts published on public channels that AI reads.

How is GEO different from SEO?

SEO aims to rank web pages high in search results; GEO aims to get AI to name your brand in the answers it generates. SEO focuses on keywords and backlinks; GEO cares more about consistent entity facts, clearly structured citable content, and matching descriptions on third-party channels (e.g. Zhihu, Toutiao, your website). They complement rather than replace each other.

What is YinYuan GEO?

YinYuan GEO is a GEO workspace SaaS for Chinese-speaking markets, targeting Chinese and global generative engines (automated prompts currently cover DeepSeek; more engines are being connected). Fixed flow: entity card → engine packs → channel publishing → fixed-prompt mention monitoring. The product is pre-launch; you can request a trial on the website.

Which AI engines does YinYuan GEO support?

Only DeepSeek is connected and runs prompts automatically today. Doubao, Qwen, Zhipu, Baidu ERNIE, Kimi, Yuanbao and global ChatGPT, Perplexity, Google AI Overviews/Gemini, Claude and Copilot are being connected and are not monitored automatically yet; you can paste real answers from them into the monitor table manually. Pack templates exist for Doubao, DeepSeek, Zhipu, Baidu ERNIE and Qwen; dedicated overseas packs are on the roadmap. YinYuan does not call any platform's publishing API — you publish content to public channels yourself.

How do I monitor whether AI mentions my brand?

YinYuan asks engines fixed prompts on a schedule (e.g. “recommend a few tools in category XX”), records whether you are named, your position and whether sources are cited, and computes this week's mention rate over filled cells. DeepSeek runs automatically today; other engines are being connected — until then you can paste real answers manually. The system never fabricates results.

How long does GEO take to work?

There is no fixed timeline, and no one can guarantee results. It depends on how competitive the category is, whether your channels are indexed by the engines, each engine's model and web-search update cycle, and whether your messaging is consistent. Establish a baseline first, then track changes weekly with the same prompt set.

Who is YinYuan GEO for?

Brands that want to be recommended in Chinese and global AI answers; SMEs going overseas or operating in Taiwan, mainland China or overseas Chinese markets; content and marketing teams; and agencies doing GEO for clients. Not for teams expecting “guaranteed rankings” or only buying traffic.

How is YinYuan GEO different from a managed GEO service?

YinYuan GEO is a self-serve tool: pack templates exist for Doubao, DeepSeek, Qwen, Zhipu and Baidu ERNIE, and automated monitoring currently covers DeepSeek; other Chinese engines (including Yuanbao and Kimi) and global engines (ChatGPT, Perplexity, Google AI Overviews/Gemini, Claude, Copilot) are being connected, with dedicated overseas packs on the roadmap. Your team runs it yourself, at a lower price than managed services.
